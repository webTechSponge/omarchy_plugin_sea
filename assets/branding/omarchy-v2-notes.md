# Omarchy-inspired branding, version 2

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Reference: [official Omarchy brand assets](https://omarchy.org/brand/), inspected on 2026-09-05. The independent identity combines a stepped wave/cable, electric plug, seafoam green and pale-yellow contacts with custom pixel lettering.

Assets: `wordmark-omarchy-v2.png` and `icon-omarchy-v2.png`. These are opaque raster PNGs. Version 1 remains available for comparison.

## Wordmark prompt

Use case: style-transfer / logo-brand. Redesign the third reference (Omarchy Plugin Sea wave-plug wordmark) with an original identity inspired by the visual language of references 1 and 2. Reference1 is Omarchy official pixel wordmark; reference2 its angular icon; use ONLY their chunky grid geometry, modular stroke weight and terminal-era attitude, do NOT copy their exact logo or exact lettering. Reference3 supplies subject concept and exact product name only; completely discard its smooth script, shaded illustration and swooshes. Create a polished wide horizontal logo on flat almost-black charcoal #151a1b. Left: compact ORIGINAL emblem built from thick pixel-grid steps: a curling sea wave/cable that ends in a recognizable two-prong electric plug. Wave resembles an open spiral, expressive asymmetrical crest with a single detached square spray pixel; plug emerges naturally from upper-right crest, square upright prongs. All contours constructed from deliberate horizontal and vertical segments, absolutely no smooth arcs. Single seafoam mint color #71ddb1 for emblem, with one tiny warm pale-yellow connector accent allowed. Right: exact text "OMARCHY" on a restrained small upper line and "PLUGIN SEA" large on lower line. Custom readable chunky pixel display letterforms, distinctive square cut-outs and subtly stepped corners, inspired by retro terminals but distinct from Omarchy exact wordmark. PLUGIN SEA in seafoam mint, OMARCHY in muted warm offwhite. Strong balanced spacing, compelling independent open-source app brand. Two-dimensional solid flat colors only; no gradients, outlines, bevels, glossy surfaces, grunge, raster noise, texture, shadows, photographic elements, extra words, watermark, or presentation board. Large clean margins. The result must have its own confident character and an immediately clear sea-plus-plugins concept.

## Icon prompt

Create the matching standalone square app icon from this exact Omarchy Plugin Sea brand design. Preserve the distinctive stepped spiral ocean wave whose inner cable ends in a two-prong electric plug, same seafoam green with two pale-yellow prongs on the same nearly-black charcoal background. Remove all text. Center the emblem alone, large with comfortable 14% margins on all sides, square composition. Maintain chunky pixel geometry and the single square spray accent, make edges crisp and flat, no smooth curved contours, no gradients or shading, no texture or speckles, no decorative frame, no words, no watermark. Faithful companion to the supplied horizontal wordmark with its own wave-and-plug identity.
