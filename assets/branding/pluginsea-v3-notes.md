# PluginSea branding, version 3

Created using the built-in image generation tool. Restores flowing ocean curves and cyan/cream colors with restrained angular terminal details. The graphical name is `PluginSea`: cream `PluginS` and turquoise `ea` share the capital S between the visual plural and Sea. Runtime display name and technical identifiers remain unchanged.

Assets: `wordmark-pluginsea-v3.png` and `icon-pluginsea-v3.png`, opaque PNGs. Earlier versions are preserved.

## Wordmark prompt

Use case: style-transfer / logo-brand. Create version3 of this app identity, a carefully balanced blend of the two supplied versions. Reference1 pixel logo is TOO rigid and too close to Omarchy. Reference2 flowing blue illustrated logo has the personality to bring back. Aim 75% flowing ocean character and25% angular terminal influence. Wide finished horizontal brand lockup on uniform dark midnight navy. Left: compact curling cyan/turquoise wave whose cable crest becomes a clearly recognizable electric plug, smooth strong curves, white foam, a few angular cut corners on plug body and wave tip subtly reference modular pixel design. Two pale cream prongs. Keep emblem flatter and simpler than reference2, more expressive than reference1, limited electric-blue/cyan palette with cream highlights, minimal droplets, no glossy3D. Right: small tracked OMARCHY upper line in restrained geometric letters. Main exact text below: "PluginSea" — ONE uninterrupted word, NO SPACE. Capital P and capital S, all other letters lowercase. Typography is original bold rounded semi-script, gently forward leaning, with a few square-cut terminals; very readable, connected visual rhythm. Critical typographic wordplay: visually group "PluginS" in warm cream as one continuous tightly kerned run, followed immediately by "ea" in turquoise; the shared capital S both finishes the visual plural "PluginS" and begins "Sea". S must be normal cap height, integrated tightly against n and e, never detached, enlarged or separated by space. Exact spelling P-l-u-g-i-n-S-e-a. Subtle wave-shaped S lower terminal, short restrained flowing underline. Strong independent app personality, ocean energy and electric plugins. No full pixel font, no all caps main word, no grunge, no checkerboard, no textures, no extra words, no slogan, no mockup, no watermark. Generous margins, clean high quality logo artwork.

## Icon prompt

Create a matching standalone square icon from this supplied PluginSea wordmark. Preserve exactly the emblem on the left: a curling turquoise/cyan ocean wave with white foam and an inner electric cable curling upward into a two-prong turquoise plug with subtly squared/angular notches. Flowing curves dominate, small angular details add a terminal-inspired touch. Remove ALL lettering and underline. Center this emblem in a square canvas with balanced 14% margins, on solid dark midnight navy, matching the source palette and clean rendering. Clear large silhouette, a few small water droplets. No extra objects, no text, no watermark. Finished companion app icon.

Approved by the user and integrated into the browse header, detail-page icon and repository README. The development installer includes both approved assets.
